TablePlus 27.4.1 build 402 Snowflake driver notices

Both Linux driver binaries identify libsnowflakeclient 2.12.0, OpenSSL
3.5.8, and zlib 1.3.1. The OpenSSL 1.1.1i literal is an encryption metadata
field in SnowflakeGCSClient.cpp at v2.12.0. The OpenSSL distribution grants
the Apache-2.0 license for its CRYPTOGAMS assembly.

The binaries also contain curl diagnostics, Boost.URL paths, cJSON type
names, picojson parser diagnostics, and the Snowflake TOML parser. Their
notices come from the dependencies referenced by libsnowflakeclient 2.12.0.
Those build inputs do not establish the exact revisions of the dependencies
in TablePlus's binaries.
